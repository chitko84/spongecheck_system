**Important:** These are stock photos from different countries. They are illustrative only; do not present them as real Sime Darby Property sites, genuine inspection evidence, or photos taken before and after maintenance. Some asset types are only approximate visual matches, not verified engineering examples.

**Download availability:** This execution environment could research licenses and photo pages but could not fetch the actual photo bytes. Accordingly, the updated application initially loads the real stock photographs over HTTPS. Run `npm run photos:download` on a network-connected computer to bundle the eleven photographs locally and rewrite the app to use those local copies; if a photo cannot be downloaded, the downloader does not claim success. See `PHOTO_INSTALLATION.md`.

**Do not replace real inspection-photo uploads** with stock images. Users still upload their own evidence or deliberately choose an example image with a demonstration label.
