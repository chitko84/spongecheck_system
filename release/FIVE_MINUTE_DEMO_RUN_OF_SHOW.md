# SpongeCheck 2.0 — Judge demonstration (target: 5 minutes)

**Style:** plain English, confident, accessible. **Show** actual clicks and record the screen. Use only a sample fictional township. Never imply real SDP asset inspections, verified rainfall forecasts or actual staff approvals.

## 0:00–0:30 — Problem, solution and mobile-first first impression

Start on the premium landing screen, ideally at **390px phone width**. Say: "Drainage facilities and living wetland habitats need maintenance, but decisions must consider both urgency and the environment. SpongeCheck helps teams report what they see, decide what to check next, and keep an accountable record of the work." Select **Explore the demo** and **Field inspector**.

## 0:30–1:20 — Report an issue in three steps

Open **Places** and choose fictional **Reed Habitat (B-02)**. Tap **Report an issue**. Select **Use example photo**, clearly showing the label **illustrative picture / example findings**. Review/edit the observations, tick confirmation, enter a short note, save the practice report. The report receives an ID and appears in **My reports**. Explain that uploaded photos do **not** automatically inherit sample annotations.

## 1:20–2:05 — Simulated rain and transparent priorities

Open **Rain simulator**, switch **Dry → Heavy**. The inspection queue and sample priorities change using deterministic demonstration rules. Open an example asset and choose **Why this needs attention**; show the actual factors, rainfall source and demo ruleset. State: "This is an explainable inspection-priority demonstration, not a flood prediction."

## 2:05–3:20 — Ecological approval safeguards

Open the saved Reed Habitat case and tap **Manage work**. Choose an intervention affecting vegetation; save the proposal. Show that an inspector cannot approve it. Switch demo role to **Nature reviewer**, open **Approvals**, enter a reason, approve. State explicitly that the roles are **simulated for the prototype** and production approval would require verified identities and local SOP review.

## 3:20–4:10 — Verified documentation, not imaginary flood prevention

Switch role to **Operations manager** and assign work. Switch to **Field inspector**, mark work started, upload an *after-work sample photo* and submit completion evidence. Switch to manager and review **Before & after**, using the comparison slider. Record a verification note. Open **Activity history** to show events and their timestamps. Don't claim that unrelated stock photos show a real physical change.

## 4:10–4:35 — Export a real record

Open the case. Download the **PDF** or HTML photo-evidence packet. Open it and briefly point to the case ID, observation details, action status, audit timeline and demo disclaimer. The exports are generated from the stored case, not hard-coded.

## 4:35–5:00 — Telegram / pilot path / finish

Open **Telegram updates**. **If a real bot is deployed and tested**, show an authorized `/inspect` photo from a phone and the arriving case. **Otherwise**, show the **Example conversation** and say "Our backend bot workflow is implemented and locally tested; live hosting and a Telegram account connection are the remaining setup steps." Finish with a one-precinct professional pilot proposal: compare inspection completeness, decision consistency and turnaround time before any field-deployment claim.

## Recording checklist

- Prepare one example case and demo persona transitions so the filmed clicks fit within five minutes. Prefer edited chapter cuts to falsely claiming a long process occurred instantly.
- Hide access keys, BotFather tokens, private chat identifiers, browser bookmarks with personal details, and unrelated tabs.
- Use zoom/large enough text, 1080p recording where available, show mobile footage plus one desktop view, and record a backup.
- Keep visible "Demo rainfall", "Example photo analysis" and "Simulated access" notices. Do not substitute a prerecorded response for real inference without labeling it.
- If anything fails during recording, explain that it is a prototype limitation. Do not create false "Live" badges or fabricated logs.
